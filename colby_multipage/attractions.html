Attractions

What To Do

Royal Saskatchewan Museum

Founded in 1906, the Royal Saskatchewan Museum is the first museum in Saskatchewan. It contains lots of natural history exhibits to help people learn about the history of Regina and the province of Saskatchewan.

Conexus Arts Centre

Opened in 1970, the Conexus Arts Centre is your place for live shows, such as plays or band performances. It is also home of the Regina Symphony Orchestra, the oldest performing orchestra in Canada.

Mosaic Stadium

Opened in 2017, located on the west border of the city, you'll find Mosaic Stadium, home of the Canadian Football League's Saskatchewan Roughriders. Every game, 25,000-30,000+ fans pour through the gates, most wearing the ever fashionable green and white colours. They shout and cheer for the whole game, and it creates an electric one of a kind atmosphere.

The best part, you can get tickets for as little as $20! So if you're looking for a fun night out with some family or friends, look no further than a Saskatchewan Roughriders game.
