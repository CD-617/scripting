Geography

What To Look For

Regina is located about 4 hours east of the Alberta/Saskatchewan border along the Trans-Canada Highway. Completely surrounded by flat prairies, you can spot it from a distance as you approach. Most noticeable is the tall-standing Mosaic Stadium, located right on the east border of the city, with the rest of downtown Regina sitting just behind.

As you enter the city, you'll first head past some smaller businesses, including some grocery stores, gas stations, and shops. Continue north across the bridge over Wascana Lake to reach the downtown and residential regions, where Mosaic Stadium is found, and once you're through there, head east to end up in the industrial zone, with larger businesses such as Walmart, Costco, Canadian Tire, and more. There is no shortage of places to check out in Regina!
