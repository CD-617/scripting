Our Local Businesses!

There are also lots of fantastic local businesses to check out in Regina! If you're looking for a good burger joint, look no further than Fuddruckers.

Fun fact, Regina has one of just two remaining Fuddruckers locations in all of Canada!

Looking for a good cup of Joe? Visit The Everyday Kitchen, a small local coffee shop downtown that sells amazing coffee and fresh donuts, baked in house every morning.

Perhaps you're looking for some collectibles. Just down the block from The Everyday Kitchen, you'll find Card Man, a hobby shop that specializes in trading card games, sports cards, and sports memorabilia.
