Regina

Capital of Saskatchewan

Welcome to Regina, the capital of the flatlands! Located in the heart of Saskatchewan, Canada, you can find lots to do here, such as visiting local businesses, shopping, visiting parks, or going to live sports and music events! You won't feel too crowded here either, with the city posting a modest population of about 263,500 people, making it just the 16th largest city in the country. Whether you are staying a while or just passing through, the residents here will welcome you as one of their own. The people here feel strong pride and connection to their city and province.
