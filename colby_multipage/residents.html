Residents

Who's Here?

Here in Regina, you will find lots of unique people. Some are born and raised here, some are visiting family or friends, others come for work or school. But one thing is consistent among them. They are all very welcoming, and many are very proud of their Saskatchewan culture.

If you're looking for loyal sports fans, look no further, as you can find Roughriders fans at every corner and in between around here. It's incredible to see how an entire city comes together over a single sports team.
